Spartan Ladder Progression Tasks
================================

Every requirement references StatsHexagon overall or axis scores, total training volume, or workout counts gathered inside the app. Each promotion lists three to five tasks while keeping the same Reach/Lift/Log phrasing shown in the Ladder UI.

- Reach 16+ Score in Shoulders
