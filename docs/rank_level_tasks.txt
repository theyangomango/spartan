Spartan Ladder Progression Tasks
================================

Every requirement references StatsHexagon overall or axis scores, total training volume, or workout counts gathered inside the app. Each promotion lists three to five tasks while keeping the same Achieve/Lift/Log phrasing shown in the Ladder UI.
